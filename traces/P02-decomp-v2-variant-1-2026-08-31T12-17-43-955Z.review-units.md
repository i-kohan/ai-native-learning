intent: Establish due-date capability: Task.dueAt, create/default/validation, and complete/reopen preserve dueAt.

acceptance:

- status and due filters compose, invalid due values return 400, and no due parameter returns the prior complete/status-filtered list.
- complete and reopen retain dueAt, and reopening a past-due completed task causes it to appear in overdue results.
- All existing task tests continue to pass.
